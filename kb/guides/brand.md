# A quiet bloom

World is One. A Poem Without Borders.

A lotus held in a fine circle. A quiet bloom, with room for a world of feeling.

The familiar lotus keeps its shape: laterite petals and rings, with warm straw accents against cream. Sea blue gives the surrounding website its cool counterpoint.

![World is One — lotus seal](assets/brand/wordmark.svg)

## Earth, sea and a little warmth

The palette follows Ahimanikya’s reference to Odisha’s natural materials: ମାଙ୍କଡ଼ା ପଥର, ନୀଳ ସାଗର and ଶୁଖା ଘଷି.

## A quiet place to stay

A little paper grain. A fine line like an engraving. A frame that gives an image room to breathe. The site should feel welcoming even when you stop reading and simply listen. The heritage detail stays at the edges; the poem and artwork keep the centre.

## Give the work room

Keep the mark’s proportions and leave clear space around it. Let paper and ink do most of the work; use laterite sparingly and straw for decoration, not small text. Headlines use Georgia; navigation and body copy use a simple system font with native-script fallbacks.

The palette belongs to the website. The artwork keeps its own colours, texture and strokes.

The original lotus seal remains the collection’s mark. Its fine lines belong with the site’s manuscript details, poetic typography and framed art. [Design roots and credits](artwork.md) · [Reuse terms](rights.md)
