## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

Let whole Odia thoughts shape the melody; use brief Odissi-informed turns without fragmenting words.

A close, warm lead; flowing phrase endings; acoustic guitar as a quiet modern bed; bamboo flute answers; restrained mardala touches. A relaxed pulse follows the line rather than chopping it into equal beats.

Use a warm youthful male lead. Choose the rhythm around the reviewed lyric; there is no collection-wide requirement for 6/8. Keep one answering instrument or voice prominent at a time, with percussion below the words.

## Emotional shape

Open almost alone. Separate the two ସାଉଁଟି phrases. Let the potter declaration hang over a held tone. Add rhythmic warmth at the mountain invitation, then return to the intimate opening texture. Keep a distinct gathering breath and a fuller potter pause, with soft instruments sustaining underneath. Add warmth without accelerating or building a dramatic crescendo. End in peaceful, hopeful contentment.
