## Cultural grounding

Nidhi Verma’s study describes bhaakh as a Dogri group vocal tradition performed without instrumental accompaniment. This new arrangement borrows the idea of shared vocal space. Its optional flute, drone and light drum are contemporary additions; it is not presented as a traditional bhaakh.

Musical reference: [Songs of Separation — A Study of Select Dogri Folk Songs, Bhaakhs](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/348960430_SONGS_OF_SEPARATION_A_STUDY_OF_SELECT_DOGRI_FOLK_SONGS_BHAAKHS).
