## Why this musical direction

Choose a soft, melodious Tamil song with restrained Carnatic shading. The tactile sound of plucked veena suits the act of shaping clay; the long smile-sowing phrase needs room to finish naturally.

## Cultural grounding

Tanjavur musician and scholar Rama Kausalya describes a musical world of Tamil and Telugu compositions, veena and violin traditions. That shared classical background allows several contemporary Tamil song approaches.
