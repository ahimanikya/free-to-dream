```text
[Intro — softly]
I am free to dream,
free to lose my way.
The North Star will lead me on.

[Verse 1]
Piece by piece,
I gather the world into my arms,
hoping to mend it,
hoping to make it beautiful.

[Verse 2]
I am a potter.

I've learned to give things form,
to bring them colour,
to sow a smile upon your face.

[Chorus]
I am free to dream,
free to lose my way.
The North Star will lead me on.
I am free to dream.

[Bridge — gently rising]
Come, let's climb the mountains.
Come, let's watch the moon.
Shoulder to shoulder with the clouds,
let's learn to fall as rain.

[Final Chorus]
I am free to dream,
free to weave your dreams with mine.
Thread by thread,
your dreams with mine.
I am free to dream.

[Soft Outro]
Your dreams with mine…
I am free to dream.
```
