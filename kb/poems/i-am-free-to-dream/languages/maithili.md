## Why this musical direction

Choose a personal, affectionate geet rather than repurposing a wedding or ritual song. The dream-weaving image should feel as close as speaking to someone beside you.

## Cultural grounding

Kailash Mishra’s IGNCA study describes a broad range of Maithili folk-song forms and their social and literary contexts. [IGNCA — Classification and Structure of Maithili Folksongs](https://ignca.gov.in/PDF_data/Classification_Structure_Maithili_FolkSongs.pdf)

The arrangement below is a creative proposal for this poem. It is not a claim to represent every tradition in the language, or to reproduce a traditional composition. Regional instruments, phrasing and song forms overlap across communities.

## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

Warm lead, soft dholak, small harmonium phrases and flute. A gentle rocking motion; lightly repeated melodic cells with clear endings and room for the polite address.

## Emotional shape

Keep the first stanza small. Let gathering add a little pulse, suspend it at the potter line, and use a soft answering voice only on the final existing refrain.
