## Why this musical direction

A graceful, carefully shaped tune can carry the lyric’s optimism without imposing a garba dance function. Aim for affection and clarity.

## Cultural grounding

NCPA’s Avinashi Avinash programme situates Gujarati sugam sangeet within a substantial tradition of composed light music. [NCPA — Avinashi Avinash](https://www.ncpamumbai.com/event/avinashi-avinash/)

The arrangement below is a creative proposal for this poem. It is not a claim to represent every tradition in the language, or to reproduce a traditional composition. Regional instruments, phrasing and song forms overlap across communities.

## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

Warm lead, violin, light harmonium, bansuri and gentle tabla. A measured lilting flow, balanced lines and a refrain with a clear arrival note.

## Emotional shape

Let the violin motif return between stanzas. Thin the texture beneath the potter declaration. Make the final refrain feel settled, as though the two dreams have found a home.
