## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

A low tamburo-like drone, bowed surando replies and restrained hand percussion. Repeated melodic lines deepen through expression rather than big chord changes.

## Emotional shape

Start with a narrow vocal range over drone. Let the refrain widen each time; pause after the potter line and make the final woven-dreams phrase the emotional arrival.
