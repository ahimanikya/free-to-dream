## Why this musical direction

## Cultural grounding

Sarma and Monteiro’s field research describes Goalpariya music’s changing performance contexts. Goalpariya is a distinct regional linguistic and musical tradition; a standard Assamese lyric with borrowed colours should not be presented as a traditional Goalpariya song. [Sarma and Monteiro — Contemporary Folk Dynamics](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0015587X.2019.1622923)

The arrangement below is a creative proposal for this poem. It is not a claim to represent every tradition in the language, or to reproduce a traditional composition. Regional instruments, phrasing and song forms overlap across communities.

## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

Dotara-like plucked figures, bamboo flute, very soft hand drum and a low sustained bass. Long breathing lines, light ornament and a gently rolling pulse.

## Emotional shape

A recurring plucked figure suggests gathering. Let the voice alone state the potter line; flute opens the bridge and the final refrain becomes almost a lullaby.
