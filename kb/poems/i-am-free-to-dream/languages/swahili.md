## Cultural grounding

Hilda Kiel’s research on Zanzibar taarab describes a Swahili coastal tradition shaped by several musical exchanges, with older small ensembles including oud, violin and percussion. This supplies a specific reference for the proposed intimate ensemble. It does not represent every Swahili-speaking region or claim a traditional taarab composition.
