## Why this musical direction

A narrative voice and bowed replies suit the poem’s journey from wandering to companionship. Give the mountain invitation a walking motion without adding scenery or words to the poem.

## Cultural grounding

Smithsonian’s Gaines of Nepal recording documents sarangi-based singing by a particular musician community. The proposed contemporary arrangement acknowledges that lineage rather than treating it as the whole of Nepali musical culture. ## Voice, rhythm and arrangement

Nepali sarangi is the principal reply; light madal and a little acoustic guitar create a steady, easy pulse. Warm lead and a modest refrain, with clear consonants.
