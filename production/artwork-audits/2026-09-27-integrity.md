# Artwork integrity audit — 27 September 2026

All 24 selected original artworks and their 22 current print editions. Superseded editions, unselected workbook archives and independent generated illustrations are outside this comparison.

## Finding

Eight current generated editions warrant correction under the artist’s cleanup-only standard. Eleven are closer but still require artist review of local marks; this is not approval or proof of no drift. Three prepared editions preserve original artwork pixels exactly before enlargement, and two works have no generated edition. No image was changed by this audit.

## What cleanup may change

Cleanup may reduce verified paper stains, dust and photographic unevenness. It must not redraw a stroke, invent a feature, clarify intentional ambiguity, change expression, fill ink, alter proportions or regenerate signatures.

Keep subject-bearing marks protected. Use the original file as the base, with reversible, local adjustments confined to identified defects. Preserve dry-brush gaps, fading, scratches made by the artist, paper fibres, washes, uncertain faces, inscriptions and signatures. Where a stain crosses a stroke, leave it unless the artist confirms what should be restored. Do not use a new generation to repair generative drift.

## Method and limits

Visual original/current comparisons at preserved aspect ratios, with repeat inspection of sensitive faces; checksum validation of all selected originals/current editions; direct pixel-region verification for three non-generative preparations.

Judgments of acceptable drift are curatorial recommendations for the artist, not measured similarity scores. Comparisons use the available digital photographs, not physical works or colour-calibrated scans. Framing/scale, camera perspective and resampling affect appearance. No pixel-identity claim is made for generated editions or enlarged JPEGs. Faint marks cannot be reliably classified as dirt without artist confirmation.

All 24 original source checksums and every current edition checksum matched the catalogue. The Grandmother and original-edition preparation scripts passed their exact artwork-region checks. The 4× JPEGs are resampled, compressed delivery files, not pixel-identical masters.
