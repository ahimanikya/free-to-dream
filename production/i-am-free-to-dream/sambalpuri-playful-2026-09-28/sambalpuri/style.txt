Sambalpuri folk ballad, gently lilting and unhurried, bamboo-flute replies over lightly played dhol, soft bass and quiet acoustic guitar; instruments sustain through clear breaths between gathering phrases and a fuller pause after the potter declaration. The bridge opens warmly without speeding up, the refrain blooms into quiet communal joy, and the duet outro settles tenderly. Warm youthful male lead with natural Sambalpuri pronunciation, compact word endings and short connected melodic turns; soft, smiling female answers from the opening, affectionate teasing and playful conversational exchanges through the verses. Female solo for the mountain-and-moon invitation; tender close-harmony duet in the final refrain and outro. Keep the pace calm and soulful; playfulness comes from vocal expression. Replies stay clear of the lead, using only supplied lyric words.
