# Poetic identity and page audit — 27 September 2026

## Direction
Preserve World is One — A Poem Without Borders and the established artistic language. The author's Utkal Project reference supplies colour influence only. Later steering asks for a subtle heritage atmosphere: a place to linger while listening. Retain manuscript details, fine leaf ornaments, warm paper and wood-and-cloth frames. Avoid destination imagery, busy effects and animations over artwork.

## Applied
- Original SVG page/wave/star symbol, monochrome version, wordmark, favicon, social-card template, palette and downloadable brand kit.
- Shared brand.css after existing frame styles: laterite emphasis, sea-blue controls, straw accents, warm paper, quiet border and wave details.
- Language pages: clearer expandable production/review notes, lighter player surface, readable links and mobile spacing; poem wording and native scripts preserved.
- Artwork detail: remove redundant self-navigation, retain print and original access, fold technical context and preparation descriptions into disclosures, maintain intact framed art and embedded download captions.
- Recording pages: provenance/credits available on demand.
- Footer: rights/privacy/cookies visible, secondary project resources grouped behind one disclosure.
- Design credits point to the current Art Journal and separate brand guide; no redundant original-gallery promotion.
- Hash navigation reveals collapsed destinations, including original art.

## Delivery
Local preview only. These changes have not been committed, pushed or published. Existing unrelated work remains in place.

## Final logo choice
After viewing both marks side by side, the author selected the original lotus seal. The header mark, favicon, monochrome mark, wordmark, social card, brand guide and downloadable kit now use the lotus. The existing earthy palette, typography and heritage styling remain. The page/wave/star proposal is superseded; a comparison copy is preserved outside the published site.

The author then requested applying the suggested colours to the lotus itself: laterite petal lines, sea-blue outer circle and straw accents on cream. Geometry and fine line weights remain intact.

Final colour refinement: retain laterite for both lotus rings with straw petals; use sea blue in the site’s controls and listening player, rather than in the seal.
