# Approved poetic asset application

Applied the author-approved visual board using the existing lotus, wave/thread, leaf border, pothi, paper, walnut and woven-cloth assets. No artwork pixels or captions were changed.

- The home story transition uses the flowing thread; the quote retains its existing wave/star, with its duplicate divider removed.
- Completed poem text ends with one small, non-interactive thread ornament. Adaptation briefs without a poem do not get a decorative ending.
- The original poem gains the existing pothi in a reserved heading area, with a smaller mobile version.
- Art Journal closing uses a wave instead of a straight border. Artwork detail frames sit against a faint warm radial light.
- Reference and contribution headings receive one short leaf detail.
- Collection-player progress uses straw gold against sea blue; pressed controls retain a distinguishable warm accent.
- Existing paper, wooden frames, cloth insets and lotus remain intact. No new animations or generated images.
